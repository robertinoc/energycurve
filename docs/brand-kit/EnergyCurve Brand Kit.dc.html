ENERGYCURVE

Shape the curve. Own the dancefloor.

The visual and verbal system for the copilot that helps DJs design better sets by analyzing the energy curve of their music.

01 — Brand Essence

A creative copilot for the energy of a set — not a replacement for the artist.

EnergyCurve turns set-building from something purely intuitive into something assisted, conscious and optimized — clear, actionable feedback adapted to context and genre.

Intelligent

Data-driven, explainable analysis. We show the why behind every score — never magic, always reasoning.

Creative

We assist the craft, we don't impose on it. The artist keeps the final say; we sharpen the instinct.

Nightlife

Built for the club. Dark, electric, high-energy — the interface feels like the room it's designing for.

Product Principles

Simple>complex
Explainable>magical
Assist>impose
Flow>individual tracks

Who it's for

Beginner & intermediate DJs (18–35) on Rekordbox, Serato or Traktor — building sets at home or small events, and looking to level up.

Secondary: semi-pro DJs optimizing sets, validating decisions and saving time.

02 — Logo

The waveform mark

A single continuous line traces a set's energy — a calm intro, a towering peak, a controlled drop, a strong close — held inside a circle. It is the product.

Primary lockup — horizontaluse most
Stacked lockup
App icon iOS · Android
Vector mark SVG · scalable
Monochrome 1-color · dark bg
Inverse 1-color · light bg

Clearspace & minimum size

Keep clear space of at least the height of the circle mark (1×) on every side. Never place the logo below 24px tall (icon) or 20px tall (horizontal mark).

Don't

✕Recolor the gradient or use off-brand hues
✕Stretch, rotate or distort the mark
✕Place the neon logo on light or busy backgrounds
✕Add drop shadows or outlines beyond the built-in glow
✕Break the wordmark weight contrast (bold ENERGY / light CURVE)

05 — Voice & Tone

Confident coach, never a critic

We speak like a trusted studio friend who knows the dancefloor: clear, encouraging, specific. We explain, we suggest — we never lecture.

Tagline

Shape the curve.
Own the dancefloor.

We say

“Your energy peaks early — try moving track 6 later to build tension.”

“Strong close. This set lands where it should.”

“Three tracks sit at the same energy — the floor may feel flat here.”

We avoid

“Your set is bad. Score: 3/10.”

“Our proprietary AI has detected suboptimal spectral entropy.”

“You must reorder these tracks now.”

ClearPlain language over jargon.
SpecificName the track, the fix, the why.
EncouragingCredit what works first.
HumanA friend at the decks, not a machine.
